# Password Reset and Account Lockout

## Resetting your password with self-service password reset

You can reset your own password at any time using self-service password reset (SSPR) at https://aka.ms/sspr. You must have registered at least two authentication methods, such as Microsoft Authenticator and a mobile phone number.

1. Go to https://aka.ms/sspr from any browser.
2. Enter your work email address and complete the captcha.
3. Verify your identity with two of your registered methods.
4. Choose a new password that meets the password policy.

## Password policy

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases made of several unrelated words are recommended. Passwords expire only if there is evidence of compromise. Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff - Contoso IT will never ask for your password.

## Account lockout

Your account is locked for 15 minutes after 10 failed sign-in attempts. Wait 15 minutes or use self-service password reset to unlock it immediately. If you did not cause the failed attempts, report it as a possible security incident by creating a P2 ticket and contacting the Security Operations Center.
