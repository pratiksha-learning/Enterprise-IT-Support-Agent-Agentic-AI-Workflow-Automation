# Outlook and Teams Troubleshooting

## Outlook is not receiving email

Check the Microsoft 365 service status page first. In Outlook, select Send/Receive and then Update Folder. If Outlook shows "Disconnected" or "Working offline", select the Send/Receive tab and turn off Work Offline. Rebuilding your Outlook profile from Control Panel, Mail, Show Profiles resolves most persistent sync problems.

## Mailbox is full

Mailboxes have a 100 GB limit. Enable the online archive by right-clicking your mailbox and selecting Archive, or delete large items from Sent Items and Deleted Items.

## Teams audio or video problems

In Teams, open Settings, Devices and make sure the correct speaker, microphone and camera are selected. Make a test call to check your devices. Close other applications that might be using the camera. Clearing the Teams cache (quit Teams and delete the contents of the Teams cache folder) resolves many performance issues.
