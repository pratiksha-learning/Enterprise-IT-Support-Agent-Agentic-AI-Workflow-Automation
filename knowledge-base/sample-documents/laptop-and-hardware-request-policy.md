# Laptop and Hardware Request Policy

## Standard laptop refresh

Employees receive a standard laptop that is refreshed every 4 years. Developers and designers may request a performance model with manager approval. Laptops are managed by Microsoft Intune and must remain encrypted with BitLocker or FileVault.

## Requesting new hardware or accessories

Standard accessories such as a mouse, keyboard, headset or a single external monitor can be requested through the IT Service Portal and do not need approval. A second monitor or non-standard hardware needs manager approval. Delivery normally takes 5 business days.

## Broken or faulty hardware

If your laptop screen flickers, the battery drains quickly, or the device does not start, create an IT ticket with category hardware. The service desk will arrange a repair or a loaner laptop within 2 business days. Do not attempt to open the device yourself.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone immediately as a P2 ticket so the device can be remotely locked and wiped. You must also inform your manager.
