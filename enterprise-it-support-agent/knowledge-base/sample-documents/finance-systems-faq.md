# Finance Systems FAQ

## Month-end close system access

The month-end close application is available only from the corporate network or VPN. During the last three business days of each month, the system freezes journal entries after 18:00 local time.

## Expense report submission

Submit expense reports in the expense tool within 30 days. Receipts above 75 USD must be attached. Reports are approved by your manager and then by Finance Operations.

## Finance SharePoint site

The Finance SharePoint site stores monthly reports and budget templates. Request the visitor role to read reports or the member role to edit budget templates through the IT Support Agent.
