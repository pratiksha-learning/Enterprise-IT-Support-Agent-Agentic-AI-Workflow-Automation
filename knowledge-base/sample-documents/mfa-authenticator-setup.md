# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Setup

All Contoso accounts require multi-factor authentication. The approved method is the Microsoft Authenticator app with number matching.

## Setting up Microsoft Authenticator

1. Install Microsoft Authenticator from the App Store or Google Play on your phone.
2. On your computer, go to https://aka.ms/mysecurityinfo and sign in.
3. Select Add sign-in method, choose Authenticator app and follow the prompts to scan the QR code.
4. Approve the test notification by entering the number shown on screen.

## New phone or lost phone

If you get a new phone, add the Authenticator app on the new phone at https://aka.ms/mysecurityinfo before wiping the old one. If you lost your phone, contact the IT Service Desk immediately so they can issue a Temporary Access Pass after verifying your identity; then register your new device.
