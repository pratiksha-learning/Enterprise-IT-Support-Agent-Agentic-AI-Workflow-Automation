# Office Printer Setup

## Adding a printer

Contoso uses Universal Print. On Windows open Settings, Bluetooth and devices, Printers and scanners, select Add device and choose the printer for your floor, for example PRN-HQ-3F. On macOS use System Settings, Printers and Scanners and select the plus button.

## Secure release printing

Print jobs are held until you tap your badge on the printer. Jobs that are not released are deleted after 24 hours.

## Common printing problems

If a print job is stuck, cancel it from the print queue and resend it. If the printer shows "offline", check that you are on the office network or connected to VPN. Paper jams and toner replacements should be reported to the facilities desk on your floor.
